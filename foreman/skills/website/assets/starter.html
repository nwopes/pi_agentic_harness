How it works

From scattered to sorted in three steps

  1. Add this week's work

    Paste a list or import from your tracker. Tidyflow groups tasks by owner automatically.

  2. Pick the three that matter

    Each person stars their top priorities. The board shows the whole team's focus at a glance.

  3. Let the week run

    Reminders, focus blocks and the Friday summary keep things moving without a check-in meeting.
